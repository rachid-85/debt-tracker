About Notebook Prototype

Page 1 (Den / Debt): Track debts in USD ($). Search, sort by amount or date, add/edit/remove persons, contact via WhatsApp directly, pay in full or partial amounts, add new debt amounts, and merge accounts.

Page 2 (Jardé): Daily shift cash flow sheet. Automatically pulls the total debt sum from Page 1 into the "Den" field. Enter Cash, Alfa, App, and Whish manually to instantly compute the grand Total.

All changes are securely saved in your device's local storage.
